# Three-Job Magnet Test

Gates a lead magnet before it ships: it must reveal an unknown problem (or its size), sample the solution, and leave the core offer as the obvious completion.

A free plugin from KAIROS AI Systems — one focused tool that does one job well for a
small business: no suite to learn, no setup project, no account required beyond your
agent host.

## What it does

Fails any one job = doesn't ship. A paid magnet must also pre-qualify. The sample is a piece of the client's fix, never a tour of internal mechanics.

## How to use it

Add this plugin to a compatible agent host (Cursor, Grok Bot, or another
agent-plugins host), then ask in plain language, for example:

> Use the Three-Job Magnet Test to help me: gates a lead magnet before it ships: it must reveal an unknown problem (or its size), sample the solution, and leave the core offer as the obvious completion — I'll paste the details.

The plugin works from the facts you give it — your services, your prices, your
records. It never invents a number, a policy, or a result; anything it projects is
labelled an estimate with its basis. Drafts that would go to a customer stay drafts
until you approve them.
